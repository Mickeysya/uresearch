- **Environment:** a throwaway worktree of `feature/ga-extension-shortcut` (`9a45624`) inside the app container, with its own SQLite database, a synchronous queue and file sessions; email went to the project's Mailpit. The dev database, the main checkout and both branches were not touched, and no application code was changed.
- **Date:** the run took place on 12 November 2026, the pinned "today", so no clock change was needed.
- **Accounts:** the standard seed (`php artisan db:seed`). Users as named in the test cases; "non-GA demo student" = student2@ (Nur Farah Adilah); "requested supervisor" = kumaran.vellu@ (Prof. Dr. Kumaran Vellu, Computing).
- **Students added:** none through sign-up or admin screens. `NureenClientCharterDemoSeeder` (an existing demo seeder) created its charter demo students, 22091001 to 22091024, for SHR-02.
- **Setup data, all through real forms, the WorkflowEngine or demo seeders** (never by writing `status` or `current_stage`):

  - GA Extension: Admin's verification of #4, #7, #8 and #9; supervisor@'s endorsement of #7 and #9, and re-endorsement of #4 after GAX-10, through the engine.
  - Supervision: kumaran.vellu@'s endorsement of #12 and #13, and chair@'s of #13, through the engine.
