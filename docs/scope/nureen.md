## Job Scope & Module Breakdown

**Project Title:** *Design and Development of an Intelligent Postgraduate Administration and Workflow Automation System with Predictive Analytics for UResearch 2.0*

### 1. Core Focus
Nureen’s scope focuses on shifting CGS from *reactive* administrative tracking to *proactive* intervention, alongside automating standard document workflows and compliance checks.

### 2. Assigned Modules (4 Modules)

#### Module 1: Attendance Record Module
* **Function:** Automates the calculation of student attendance percentages from UTrace data, eliminating manual Excel verification. There is no live connection to UTrace, because this FYP has no access to UTP systems: data arrives as a CSV or XLSX file uploaded by CGS staff, or through an automated sync (inbox folder or push endpoint). A live API remains future work.
* **Key Innovation (Predictive Analytics):** Implements an **Early Warning System** (based on the Gradual At-Risk / GAR model concept). It predicts which students are on track to fall below the mandatory **80% minimum attendance threshold** *before* it happens.
* **Workflow:** 
  * System calculates attendance and flags at-risk students, using a rule (below 80%, or declining in each of the last two periods and within 5 points of 80%) and a least-squares projection of end-of-semester attendance.
  * Sends personalized, proactive alerts to the student and supervisor.
  * Adds the student to a CGS "At-Risk" dashboard list. Only CGS sees the projected figure; students see an "Attendance Action" card based on their own session counts.
  * Allows students to file an attendance appeal directly through the portal, which routes to CGS staff for review.

#### Module 2: GA (Graduate Assistant) Extension Module
* **Function:** Manages applications for extending Graduate Assistantships.
* **Workflow (realigned with CGS, 2026-10-31 — see §5):**
  * System performs **automated document completeness validation** prior to submission (three required uploads: IC or passport/visa copy, admission and offer letter, and supervisor's supporting letter), preventing incomplete forms from reaching CGS. Stage gate proof is optional at submission, and the GA Extension Form itself is now generated by the system rather than uploaded.
  * Routes the validated application through CGS's confirmed UResearch process by default: Supervisor (endorses) $\rightarrow$ Non-Executive CGS (final decision). The Senior Director CGS no longer has a decision stage here; they are emailed every outcome for information only.
  * A config switch (`nureen.ga_extension.final_approver`) can restore the original three-step chain — Supervisor $\rightarrow$ CGS Staff (intermediate review) $\rightarrow$ Senior Director CGS (final) — without a code change.
  * Triggers automated email notifications to the student at every step, with the endorsing supervisor copied on the final decision.

#### Module 3: Supervision Module
* **Function:** Manages Main and Co-Supervisor appointment requests.
* **Workflow (realigned with CGS, 2026-10-31 — see §5):**
  * Student submits a request naming the supervisor, the appointment role (Main or Co-Supervisor) and type (New or Existing), with required documentation.
  * System checks completeness and forwards to the named Supervisor for endorsement.
  * Upon Supervisor endorsement, it goes to the **Chair of Department** (of the student's own department) for approval — a department-scoped role, not a university-wide one. A config switch (`nureen.supervision.co_supervisor_approver`) can send Co-Supervisor requests to the Dean of PGR instead; Main Supervisor requests always go to the Chair.
  * By default, Non-Executive CGS then previews the draft letter and either endorses or rejects it (changed after the 2026-10-31 realignment — see §6). Endorsing issues the appointment letter (PDF, with a unique reference number) in the endorser's own name. This final approval sets the student's supervisor record for a Main appointment only, and never touches it for a Co-Supervisor appointment. Every issued letter stays visible to Non-Executive CGS, who can revoke it with a mandatory reason.
  * A config switch (`nureen.supervision.cgs_stage`) selects the mode: `'endorse'` (default, as above); `'automatic'`, where the Chair's approval is final and the letter is issued at once, naming no CGS endorser; or `'manual'`, where CGS makes an eligibility decision after the Chair and the letter is generated on that approval.
* **Key Innovation:** If rejected, the student receives specific feedback and the named supervisor is copied. If the approval process stalls at either the Supervisor's or the Chair's desk, the system **automatically sends reminder escalations** to the pending approver, looping in CGS Staff after repeated reminders.

#### Module 4: GA/GRA Certification Letter Module
* **Function:** Digitizes the endorsement workflow for issuing GA/GRA completion certificates.
* **Workflow (realigned with CGS, 2026-10-31 — see §5):**
  * Student picks GA or GRA and the form shows only the fields relevant to that type — course details for a GA, research project details for a GRA — each independently configurable without a code change.
  * Routes first to the **student's own named supervisor** for review, then to CGS Staff, then to the Senior Director CGS for final digital endorsement. Submission is blocked up front with a clear message if the student has no supervisor on record, rather than creating an application nobody can review.
  * CGS staff may optionally see an automatic appointment-record check (off by default — not part of CGS's confirmed process, config-gated).
* **Key Innovation (Automated PDF Generation):** Upon final approval, the system integrates **Dompdf** to automatically generate, format, and dispatch the official PDF certification letter, which the student can instantly download.

### 3. Key Technical Contributions
* **State Machine Diagram:** Designed the universal application lifecycle state machine (Submitted $\rightarrow$ Under Review $\rightarrow$ Approved/Rejected) used across the GA Extension, Supervision, and Certification modules. As implemented, GA Extension and Certification requests can also be saved as drafts before submission, and CGS can return a GA Extension to the supervisor.
* **Predictive Engine Logic:** Tasked with developing the PHP/MySQL logic to query historical attendance data and flag at-risk students for the dashboard. As implemented, this is a rule plus a least-squares projection of end-of-semester attendance, not a risk probability. It can be evaluated with `attendance:backtest`.
* **UI/UX Design:** Designed the Student Dashboard (showing attendance, the "Attendance Action" card and active applications) and the CGS Staff Dashboard (showing document verification queues and at-risk alerts).

### 4. Scope Evolution
During the proposal defense, the scope included broad AI concepts. Through deep-dive interviews with CGS staff (En Zulkifly), the scope was refined to focus on **rule-based predictive analytics** and **automated document validation**, ensuring the features were practically implementable within the FYP2 timeline using PHP and MySQL.

### 6. Changes Since the Realignment (as of 2026-11-06)

#### 6.1 Changes to the §5 decisions

* **Supervision's CGS step:** the default is now `'endorse'` rather than `'automatic'`. CGS endorses the draft letter before it is issued, so an official letter only ever names an endorser who actually endorsed it. `'automatic'` and `'manual'` are still available, and in-flight applications keep the mode they were submitted under.
* **Client Charter:** `nureen.charter_days` is now in use. It drives stage due dates, overdue markers in approver queues, stall reminders for all three approval modules, students' expected decision dates, and the processing-time report.

#### 6.2 Implemented beyond the original scope

**Attendance Record**
* Backtesting of the rule and projection against known outcomes (`attendance:backtest`).
* A configurable window for when the projection may flag a student (by default, from the 4th period up to 60% of the semester).
* Approved annual leave tracking against the Postgraduate Handbook 2025 allowance; going over the allowance warns CGS.
* A switch to monitor only full-time research students (`nureen.attendance.scope`; off by default).
* Required appeal documents (justification letter and monthly activity breakdown); an approved appeal excuses the sessions it covers.

**GA Extension**
* The official form (UTP/CGS/001-A2) generated as a PDF from the student's answers.
* Terms and Conditions and Handbook checks shown to approvers: duration, stage gates, eligibility and candidacy limit. These warn only; a backdated start is refused at submission.
* CGS can return an application to the supervisor, and records the form's CGS remarks section.
* Identity and bank account numbers encrypted at rest and masked outside the CGS stage.

**Supervision**
* A PGFAC approval memo is required when a GA changes Main Supervisor.

**GA/GRA Certification Letter**
* The form aligned with CGS's official online form, with a pre-filled course-owner attachment to download.
* Eligibility warnings that never block submission, because a former GA may still need a letter.
* Issued certificates can be revoked with a reason.

**Across the modules**
* Saved drafts for GA Extension and Certification (My Drafts).
* A student page of Expected Decision Dates, based on the Client Charter.
* A processing-time report for CGS: decisions made within charter, average working days, and overdue applications.
* Stuck Reviews: CGS sees requests no account can act on, and can reassign a GA Extension or Certification to another supervisor.
* Public verification pages for issued letters (`/verify/certificate/…` and `/verify/appointment/…`).
* In-app document preview for approvers, and a read-only staff view of an application for email links.
