Research students get automatic reminders before their candidacy ends.

**CGS process.** Research students get an automatic reminder by e-mail and bell **3, 2 and 1 month before the expiry date**, sent at 08:00.
- A reminder is sent once; if several are overdue only the latest goes out.
- They stop when your Softbound submission is recorded, when you complete or are made inactive, when you are dismissed, and while an appeal is in review. They continue if the appeal is returned or decided.
- Coursework students are not reminded.

Ask me "When will I get my next reminder?" to see yours.
