After a return (CGS process): the appeal comes back to you with the reasons. Fix what was asked and resubmit before your candidacy expires; it goes back to the step that returned it (after a Dean return, CGS re-checks first). If you do not resubmit before the expiry date, it is closed as expired.

After a rejection (CGS process): a Dean rejection is final. No further appeal can be submitted and your expiry date stays as it was. You can still submit the Softbound thesis before the expiry date.

The Handbook says that exceeding the maximum duration of candidacy leads to dismissal, and that an appeal for reinstatement goes to the Registry.

Source: CGS process; Postgraduate Student Handbook 2025, sections 4.3 p.19 and 4.4 p.20 (Handbook; verify page).
