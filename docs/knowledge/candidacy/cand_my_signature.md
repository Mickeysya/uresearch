CGS process. Supervisors, Department Chairs, the Dean and CGS sign the decisions they make. Open My Signature (in the menu under Actions, or the link on the Approval Portal), upload a PNG or JPEG of your signature and save; you can remove it there too. Your signature is stamped on the decisions you make and on the approval memo. A CGS user's signature is optional (a text-only stamp is used without one).
