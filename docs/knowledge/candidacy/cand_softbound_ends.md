**The Handbook says:** for a research programme the candidacy period runs until you submit the soft-bound thesis, and the soft-bound thesis must be submitted for viva voce before the end of candidature.

**CGS process:** once your Softbound submission is recorded, your candidacy reminders stop and you are not a dismissal candidate.

Source: Postgraduate Student Handbook 2025, sections 2.1 p.7 and 5.3 p.30 (Handbook; verify page).
