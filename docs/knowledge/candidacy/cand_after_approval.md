When the Dean approves your appeal, these things happen.

CGS process. After the Dean approves:
• Your expiry date is recalculated (the approved months are added to your current expiry date).
• The approval memo is e-mailed to you, and you are notified by bell. The memo can be downloaded from the appeal page and can be checked with its verification code.
• Your remaining extension months go down by the approved months.
• Reminders restart for the new expiry date.

Source: CGS process (after approval).
