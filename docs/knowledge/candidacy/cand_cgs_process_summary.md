This is the whole CGS process for candidacy appeals and dismissals, in order.

**CGS process, end to end.**
1. Students appeal for more time on or before the expiry date: Supervisor, Department Chair, CGS verification, Dean. Maximum extension {{cand_max_extension_months}} months in total. A Dean rejection is final.
2. After approval the expiry date is recalculated and the memo is e-mailed.
3. Reminders go to research students 3, 2 and 1 month before the expiry date at 08:00, and stop at Softbound submission, completion, dismissal, inactive, or while an appeal is in review.
4. After expiry without Softbound (and no appeal in review) the student appears on the Dismissal List. CGS exports it, the Registry processes it outside this system, and the result is uploaded; those students are dismissed.
5. The Handbook gives the Registry the reinstatement appeals after dismissal.

Source: CGS process; Postgraduate Student Handbook 2025, sections 4.3 and 4.4 (Handbook; verify page).
