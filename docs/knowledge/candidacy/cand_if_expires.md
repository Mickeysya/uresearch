**The Handbook says:** exceeding the maximum duration of candidacy leads to dismissal. The decision is at the discretion of the University Examination Committee and Senate. An appeal for reinstatement or re-admission after dismissal goes to the Registry. No GA/GRA allowance is paid if the candidacy period is exceeded.

**CGS process (what happens here):** after the expiry date, if no Softbound has been submitted and no appeal is in review, you appear on the dismissal list. CGS exports the list, the Registry processes it outside this system, and the result is uploaded; those students are then marked as dismissed and are told by e-mail.

Submit the Softbound thesis, or an appeal on or before the expiry date, to avoid this.

Source: Postgraduate Student Handbook 2025, sections 4.3 p.19, 4.4 p.20 and 7.4 p.38 (Handbook; verify page); the list and upload: CGS process.
