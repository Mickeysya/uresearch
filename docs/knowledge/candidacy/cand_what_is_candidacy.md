What candidacy is. Your candidacy is the period in which you are registered to complete your research programme. For a research programme it runs from your first registered semester until you submit the soft-bound thesis.

The Handbook says (maximum and minimum duration of candidacy):
• PhD: full-time minimum 36 and maximum 96 months; part-time minimum 48 and maximum 120 months.
• Master by research: full-time minimum 24 and maximum 48 months; part-time minimum 36 and maximum 72 months.
• Master by coursework: minimum 12 and maximum 36 months. MBA Energy Management: minimum 18 and maximum 36 months.
• Deferment, a change of study mode and a change of programme can change the candidacy period.

CGS process: your own expiry date and the months of extension you have left are on My candidacy; ask me "When does my candidacy expire?".

Source: Postgraduate Student Handbook 2025, section 2.1, p.7 (Handbook; verify page). Expiry date: your candidacy record.
