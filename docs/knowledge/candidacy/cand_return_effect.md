CGS process. When you return an appeal it goes back to the student with the reasons you chose (and your remark). The student fixes it and resubmits, and it comes back to your step (after a Dean return, CGS re-checks first). If the student does not resubmit before the candidacy expires, the appeal is closed as expired. A return needs at least one reason; "Other" needs a remark.

Source: CGS process (returns).
