**CGS process, the Dismissal List.** A student is on it when today is after the expiry date and: no Softbound is submitted, no appeal is in review, the student is not completed, inactive or already dismissed, and the programme is research. A Dean rejection alone does not put a student on the list; an appeal still in review on the expiry day keeps the student off it until it is decided. The list is live: a student leaves it when dismissed, and students the Registry did not confirm stay on it.

**The Handbook says** exceeding the maximum duration of candidacy leads to dismissal.

Source: CGS process (Dismissal List); Postgraduate Student Handbook 2025, section 4.3, p.19 (Handbook; verify page).
