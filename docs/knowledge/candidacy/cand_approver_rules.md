What you can do at your step depends on your role in the approval route.

CGS process, by step:
• Supervisor, Department Chair and CGS: Approve (the appeal moves on) or Return (it goes back to the student with at least one reason). They cannot reject.
• Dean: Approve, Return or Reject. A rejection needs at least one reason from the Dean's list and is final.
• You can only act on an appeal that is waiting for you (Pending my action); you see only the appeals named on you.
• After a Dean return, CGS re-checks before it returns to the Dean.

Source: CGS process (approver rules).
