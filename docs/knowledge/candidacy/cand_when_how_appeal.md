The Handbook says: a student who needs more than the maximum period may apply to CGS for an extension, on the recommendation of the supervisor (or programme manager) or the Department Chair, before or on the day the candidacy ends. The Graduate Studies Academic Committee deliberates, and the University may approve or reject.

CGS process (how to do it here):
1. Open New appeal (only shown when you are eligible) and fill in the form: the months requested (between 1 and what you have left), your progress and the reason.
2. Upload the existing university appeal form as a supporting document (see "What documents do I need for an appeal?").
3. Choose your supervisor from your department and submit. You cannot withdraw an appeal after you submit it.
4. It goes Supervisor, Department Chair, CGS verification, Dean. You are told by e-mail and bell at each decision.

Source: Postgraduate Student Handbook 2025, section 2.1.1, p.8 (Handbook; verify page). The steps: CGS process (Study Candidacy Appeal).
