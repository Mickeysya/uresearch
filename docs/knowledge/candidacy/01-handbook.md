# Study Candidacy: Postgraduate Student Handbook 2025

Only the Handbook items that belong to Study Candidacy. Each `## cand_...` section is shown to every role as written. Anything else in the Handbook (RPD and
RCS deadlines, viva, allowances, probation and so on) is outside this assistant.

## cand_what_is_candidacy

Candidacy is the period in which you are registered to complete your programme. For a research programme it runs from your first registered semester until you
submit the soft-bound thesis. Maximum and minimum duration:
• PhD: full-time minimum 36 and maximum 96 months; part-time minimum 48 and maximum 120 months.
• Master by research: full-time minimum 24 and maximum 48 months; part-time minimum 36 and maximum 72 months.
• Master by coursework: minimum 12 and maximum 36 months. MBA Energy Management: minimum 18 and maximum 36 months.
Deferment, a change of study mode and a change of programme can change the period.

Source: Postgraduate Student Handbook 2025, section 2.1, p.7 (and pp.7-8 for the changes).

## cand_hb_extension

A student who needs more than the maximum period applies to CGS for an extension, on the recommendation of the supervisor or programme manager, or the Chair of
Department. The application is made before or on the day the candidacy ends. The Graduate Studies Academic Committee deliberates, and the University may approve or reject.

Source: Postgraduate Student Handbook 2025, section 2.1.1, p.8.

## cand_deferment_mode_programme

• Deferment with a medical reason is not counted in the study period; a deferment without a medical reason is counted. Apply in UCampus; the Registrar approves.
• A change of study mode and a change of programme are applied in UCampus and approved by the Registrar, and can change the candidacy period.
After the Registrar approves, CGS updates the expiry date in the system if needed.

Source: Postgraduate Student Handbook 2025, section 2.5 pp.12-13, section 2.6 p.13 and section 2.7 pp.13-14; the expiry update by CGS: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_hb_dismissal

Exceeding the maximum duration of candidacy leads to dismissal. The decision is at the discretion of the University Examination Committee and the Senate. An
appeal for reinstatement is made in writing to the Registry, outside this system.

Source: Postgraduate Student Handbook 2025, section 4.3 p.19 and section 4.4 p.20.

## cand_softbound_ends

The soft-bound thesis must be submitted for viva voce before the end of candidature. Submitting it ends the candidacy period, so the candidacy reminders stop and
the student is not a dismissal candidate.

Source: Postgraduate Student Handbook 2025, section 5.3 item ix, p.30 (and section 2.1, p.7).
