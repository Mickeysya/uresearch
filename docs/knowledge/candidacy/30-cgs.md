# Study Candidacy: answers for CGS

Each `## cand_...` section is what CGS staff are told. A CGS user can read every appeal and acts at the CGS Verification step.

## cand_see_all_appeals

Open Candidacy Management > Operations. It lists every appeal, with the cards (In Progress, Returned, Approved, Rejected), the tabs, and the filters Search, Department,
Status, Current Step, Months Requested, Waiting At Least and Round; each row opens the appeal read-only. The Approval Portal also has a "View all appeals" link to
the same list. The Approval Portal itself shows only the appeals waiting at your CGS Verification step (Pending my action) and the ones you decided (Decided by me).

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_how_decide

Your step is CGS Verification. Open the Approval Portal, press Review on an appeal in Pending my action, check the form and documents, then press Verify (it goes
to the Dean) or Return to student (at least one reason; "Other" needs a remark). You cannot reject; only the Dean can. A signature is optional for CGS (a text-only
stamp is used without one). After a Dean return the appeal comes back to you to re-check before it returns to the Dean.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_approver_rules

At CGS Verification you can Verify or Return to student. Supervisor and Department Chair approve or return; the Dean approves, returns or rejects (final, with a
second confirmation). CGS reads every appeal on Operations but decides only at its own step.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_my_signature

Open My Signature (the link on the Approval Portal), upload a PNG or JPEG (up to 1 MB) and press Save signature; Remove my signature removes it. For CGS the
signature is optional: without one, a text-only stamp is used.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_when_how_appeal

Students ask for more time with Submit an Appeal on My Candidacy: Step 1 Department and supervisor, Step 2 Research and extension (Sections A to D), Step 3
Documents, Step 4 Review and submit. It must be submitted on or before the expiry date, and the total extension is at most {{cand_max_extension_months}} months. The
appeal then goes Supervisor, Department Chair, CGS Verification, Dean.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_required_documents

Each appeal has the official appeal form (.xlsx, required) and up to {{cand_max_supporting}} supporting documents (.xlsx, PDF, .docx), each at most {{cand_max_file_mb}} MB.
At CGS Verification check the form is the right version and the documents are present and clear; if not, Return to student with the reason "Appeal form missing or
wrong version", "Required documents missing" or "Documents unclear".

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_approval_route

Student, Supervisor, Department Chair, CGS Verification (your step), Dean. A return goes to the student and, after resubmission, back to the step that returned it; after a
Dean return it comes to CGS first. Only the Dean can reject, and that is final. Appeals not resubmitted by the expiry date are closed as expired at 07:30.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_return_effect

A return from CGS Verification goes to the student with your reasons and remark. After the student resubmits, it comes back to CGS Verification. If the student does
not resubmit before the candidacy expires, the appeal is closed as expired.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_return_reasons

CGS Verification reasons: appeal form missing or wrong version, required documents missing, documents unclear, information inconsistent with records, other (remark
required). The Dean's return reasons are clarification needed, supporting documents insufficient, justification insufficient and other; the Dean's reject reasons
are listed on the Dean's reject dialog.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_dean_rejection

A Dean rejection is final: no further appeal for that candidacy and the expiry date stays. The student is told by e-mail with the reasons. A Dean rejection alone does
not put the student on the Dismissal List; that happens only after the expiry date with no Softbound and no appeal in review.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_resubmit

When a student resubmits a returned appeal it returns to the step that returned it. If it was a Dean return, CGS Verification re-checks it before it goes back to the Dean.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_after_approval

When the Dean approves, the expiry date is recalculated and the memo is e-mailed to the student. The memo file is named `<appeal no>_<Student Name>_<Student ID>_Memo -
Appeal for Extension.pdf` and can be downloaded from the appeal page.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_notifications

Students get an e-mail and bell at each approval, return, rejection and the final approval (with the memo), plus the expiry reminders. Approvers get an e-mail and
bell when an appeal is pending their action.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_reminders

Research students get a reminder by e-mail and bell 3, 2 and 1 month before the expiry date at 08:00. Each is sent once; if several
are overdue only the latest goes. Reminders stop at Softbound submission, completion, inactive status or dismissal, and pause while an appeal is in review; after
an approved appeal a fresh cycle starts for the new expiry date. Coursework students are not reminded. There are three message variants. Not available yet: a
reminder log page, and 3-day and 1-day urgent reminders; the Insights page shows how many reminders were sent.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_if_expires

After the expiry date, a research student with no Softbound and no appeal in review appears on the Dismissal List. CGS exports it, the Registry processes it outside
this system, and the result is uploaded on Dismissal Upload. The Handbook says exceeding the maximum duration leads to dismissal (section 4.3, p.19).

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS); Postgraduate Student Handbook 2025, section 4.3 p.19.

## cand_waiting_colours

The dot on a waiting appeal is grey under 5 days, amber 5 to 9 days and red 10 days or more at the current step. Use Waiting At Least on Operations to filter.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_portal_guide

Approval Portal for CGS: Pending my action (appeals at CGS Verification), Decided by me, and the "View all appeals" link to Operations. Filters: Search, Department,
Months Requested, Waiting At Least, Round.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_cgs_operations_guide

Candidacy Management > Operations: the cards (In Progress, Returned, Approved, Rejected) filter the list when clicked, and the tabs split it the same way. Narrow it with
Search (appeal number, student name or ID), Department, Status, Current Step, Months Requested, Waiting At Least and Round. Each row opens the appeal read-only. The
page refreshes by itself every 15 seconds (Pause updates stops it). Not available yet: Excel export of the Operations list.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_cgs_insights_guide

Candidacy Insights is operational monitoring from the known expiry dates: the forecast is a projection, not a prediction. The population is research students whose
expiry date has not passed and who are not dismissed, completed or inactive. Status categories, in priority order: Softbound submitted, Appeal in review, Appeal
returned, No action yet (high risk). Bands: up to 1 month, 1 to 2 months, 2 to 3 months. Clicking a bar highlights it in every chart and the student list.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_cgs_dismissal_list_guide

Candidacy Management > Dismissal List shows a student when today is after the expiry date and: no Softbound is submitted, no appeal is in review, the student is not
completed, inactive or already dismissed, and the programme is research. A student leaves the list when dismissed; students the Registry did not confirm stay on it.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_cgs_export_registry

On the Dismissal List filter if needed and press Export to Excel (it exports exactly what the filters show, and records the export). Send the file to the Registry,
which processes it outside this system. Then upload the Registry's file on Dismissal Upload (see "How do I upload the Registry's result?").

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_cgs_registry_upload

Open Dismissal Upload, choose the file the Registry returned (.xlsx or .csv with a "Student ID" column) and press Preview. The preview shows Will be dismissed, Not a
current candidate, Already dismissed, Unknown ID and Duplicate. Check it, then press Confirm and dismiss: the students still on the list are marked dismissed and
told by e-mail and bell at once. Students not in the file stay on the list.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_cgs_process_summary

1. Students appeal on or before the expiry date: Supervisor, Department Chair, CGS Verification, Dean; maximum {{cand_max_extension_months}} months in total; a Dean rejection is final.
2. After approval the expiry date is recalculated and the memo is e-mailed.
3. Reminders at 3, 2 and 1 month; they stop at Softbound submission.
4. After expiry with no Softbound and no appeal in review the student is on the Dismissal List; CGS exports it, the Registry processes it, CGS uploads the result.
5. Reinstatement appeals after dismissal go in writing to the Registry (Handbook section 4.4, p.20).

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS); Postgraduate Student Handbook 2025, section 4.4 p.20.

## cand_cgs_not_built

Not available yet: Excel export of the Operations list, a reminder log page, 3-day and 1-day urgent reminders, and a page where CGS changes an expiry date by hand. Today
expiry dates change through an approved appeal; for deferment, change of mode or change of programme the Registrar approves and CGS updates the date.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).
