# Study Candidacy: answers for approvers (Supervisor / Programme Manager, Department Chair, Dean)

Each `## cand_...` section is what an approver is told. Students' pages and CGS pages are not described here.

## cand_see_all_appeals

Open the Approval Portal. The tabs are Pending my action (waiting for you now), Ongoing (you are named on them, not waiting for you) and History (decided).
You see only the appeals you are named on, not every appeal in the university.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_how_decide

Open the Approval Portal, press Review on an appeal in Pending my action, read it, then:
• Supervisor / Programme Manager and Department Chair: Approve (it moves on) or Return to student (at least one reason; "Other" needs a remark).
• Dean: Approve, Return to student, or Reject (final). Reject needs a reason and a second confirmation (Confirm rejection). When approving, the Dean chooses the months to approve from a list that runs from 1 to the student's remaining allowance, with the requested months preselected. A different number needs a short reason; the reason is shown to approvers and CGS on the appeal page and in the history, never to the student and not on the memo.
You must have a signature saved on My Signature before you can approve.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_approver_rules

• You act only on appeals Pending my action, and see only appeals named on you.
• Supervisor / Programme Manager and Department Chair: Approve or Return. Dean: Approve, Return or Reject (final).
• A return needs at least one reason; "Other" needs a remark.
• A signature is required to approve (My Signature).
• After a Dean return, CGS re-checks before it comes back to the Dean.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_my_signature

Open My Signatures (the link in the sidebar and on the Approval Portal), find the Study Candidacy Appeal section, upload a PNG or JPEG of your signature (up to 1 MB) and press Upload signature (Replace signature when one is on file); Remove takes it
away. Your signature is stamped on your decisions and on the approval memo. Supervisors / Programme Managers, Department Chairs and the Dean must have one to approve.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_approval_route

An appeal goes Student, Supervisor / Programme Manager, Department Chair, CGS Verification, Dean. Your step sees it only when it is your turn. Each step approves it or returns it to the
student; only the Dean can reject. After the student resubmits a returned appeal it comes back to the step that returned it.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_return_effect

When you return an appeal it goes to the student with your reasons and remark. The student fixes it and resubmits and it
comes back to you, the same approver who returned it (a Dean return goes to CGS for a re-check first, then back to the Dean). If the student does not resubmit before the candidacy expires it is closed as expired.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_return_reasons

Supervisor / Programme Manager and Department Chair reasons: unclear or insufficient justification, research progress not demonstrated, publication list incomplete, RCS status or
date missing, requested duration not justified, wrong Supervisor / Programme Manager or department, supporting documents missing, other. Dean return reasons: clarification needed,
supporting documents insufficient, justification insufficient, other. Dean reject reasons: progress insufficient, completion plan not realistic, documents
insufficient or invalid, reason for delay not acceptable, previous extension not used as planned, other (remark required).

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_dean_rejection

Only the Dean can reject. Reject (final) needs at least one reason and a second confirmation dialog (Confirm rejection). A rejection is final: the student cannot
appeal again for this candidacy and the expiry date stays. The student and the people on the appeal are told by e-mail and bell.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_resubmit

After a student resubmits a returned appeal it comes back to the same approver who returned it and shows again in your Pending my action. After a Dean return CGS
re-checks first, then it goes back to the Dean.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_required_documents

On the appeal page you can open the official appeal form (.xlsx) and up to {{cand_max_supporting}} supporting documents. If something is missing or unclear, return
the appeal with the reason "Supporting documents missing" (or the reason that fits).

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_portal_guide

Approval Portal tabs: Pending my action, Ongoing, History. Filters: Search, Department, Months Requested, Waiting At Least and Round. Press Review to open an appeal.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_waiting_colours

The dot in Pending my action shows how long the appeal has waited at your step: grey under 5 days, amber 5 to 9 days, red 10 days or more.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_notifications

You get an e-mail and bell when an appeal is pending your action. The people named on an appeal are also told by e-mail when it is returned to the student or rejected.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_after_approval

After the Dean approves (for the months the Dean chose, which may differ from the months requested), the student's expiry date is recalculated and the memo "Memo - Appeal for Extension" is e-mailed to the student; it carries the signatures of
the approvers. The appeal moves to History.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_dean_adjusts_months

The Dean, and only the Dean, may approve fewer months than the student requested: at least 1 and no more than the student's remaining months. When the number differs from the request the Dean must give a short reason (at least 5 characters). You can see the requested and approved months and the reason in the appeal details; the student does not see the reason and it is not printed on the memo.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).

## cand_approver_remarks

When you approve you may add an optional remark. When you return or reject you tick at least one reason, and a remark is required when you tick "Other". The student sees the reasons and remark of a return. There is no separate comment thread on an appeal.

Source: UResearch Study Candidacy design (confirmed with CGS).
