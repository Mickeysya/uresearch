Deferment, a change of study mode and a change of programme can all affect your candidacy period.

The Handbook says:
• Deferment with a medical reason is not counted in the study period; a deferment without a medical reason is counted. It is applied for through UCampus and approved by the Registrar.
• A change of study mode and a change of programme are approved by the Registrar, and can change your candidacy period.

CGS process: these changes are not part of the appeal. After the Registrar approves, CGS adjusts the expiry date if needed (to be confirmed with CGS); ask CGS or the Registry for your own case.

Source: Postgraduate Student Handbook 2025, sections 2.1, 2.5 pp.12-13, 2.6 and 2.7 (Handbook; verify page).
