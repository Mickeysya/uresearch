Every appeal has one of these statuses, and each one tells you who has to act next.

**Appeal statuses (CGS process):**
- **Draft**: started, not submitted yet; nobody has it.
- **With Supervisor / With Department Chair / With CGS / With Dean**: waiting for that person or team to act.
- **Returned**: sent back to the student with reasons; fix and resubmit.
- **Approved**: the Dean approved; the expiry date is recalculated and the memo is e-mailed.
- **Rejected (final)**: the Dean rejected; no further appeal and the expiry date stays.
- **Closed expired**: it was returned and not resubmitted before the candidacy expired.

Source: CGS process (appeal statuses).
