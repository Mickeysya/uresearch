These are the quick answers students and approvers ask for most often.

Quick answers (CGS process unless marked):
• When does my candidacy end? On your expiry date; ask me for yours.
• How long can I extend? Up to {{cand_max_extension_months}} months in total.
• Can I appeal on my expiry day? Yes, on or before it.
• Who decides? The approvers; only the Dean can reject.
• Can I withdraw an appeal? No, not after submitting.
• Will I get reminders? Yes, 3, 2 and 1 month before, at 08:00.
• What if my candidacy expires? The Handbook says it leads to dismissal; appeals for reinstatement go to the Registry.

Source: CGS process; Postgraduate Student Handbook 2025, sections 2.1.1, 4.3 and 4.4 (Handbook; verify page).
