The Candidacy Insights page is for operational monitoring of the known expiry dates.

CGS process, Candidacy Insights. Operational monitoring from the known expiry dates; the forecast is a projection from known expiry dates, not a prediction.
• Population: research students with an expiry date that has not passed, not dismissed, completed or inactive.
• Status category: Softbound submitted, Appeal in review, Appeal returned, No action yet (high risk), in that order of priority.
• Bands: up to 1 month, 1 to 2 months, 2 to 3 months, using the reminder milestones' month arithmetic.
• Clicking a bar highlights it in every chart and the student list; the filters narrow everything.

Source: CGS process (Insights definitions); docs/candidacy-insights.md.
