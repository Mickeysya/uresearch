An appeal is approved step by step, and each step has its own person or team.

**CGS process.** An appeal goes in this order: Student, Supervisor, Department Chair, CGS verification, Dean.
- The Supervisor, the Department Chair and CGS can **Approve** or **Return**.
- Only the **Dean** can **Reject**, and a rejection is final.
- A return goes back to the student, and after the student resubmits it goes back to the step that returned it. After a Dean return, CGS re-checks first.
- Approvers sign with their own signature (My Signature).

**The Handbook says** that the Graduate Studies Academic Committee deliberates an extension and the University may approve or reject; here the Dean of Postgraduate and Research decides (to be confirmed with CGS).

Source: CGS process (approval route); Postgraduate Student Handbook 2025, section 2.1.1, p.8 (Handbook; verify page).
