These are the rules that apply to every appeal for more candidacy time.

Appeal rules (CGS process):
• The student must have an active candidacy that has not expired, and no appeal already in progress.
• Submit on or before the expiry date (the expiry day itself is allowed).
• The maximum extension is {{cand_max_extension_months}} months in total.
• Only one appeal at a time; no withdrawal after submission.
• Route: Student, Supervisor, Department Chair, CGS verification, Dean. Only the Dean can reject, and a rejection is final.

The Handbook says an extension is applied for before or on the day the candidacy ends, on the recommendation of the supervisor or Department Chair.

Source: CGS process; Postgraduate Student Handbook 2025, section 2.1.1, p.8 (Handbook; verify page).
