**CGS process.** An appeal must be submitted on or before your expiry date. The expiry day itself is allowed; the day after is too late. Appeals are not accepted after the candidacy has expired.

**The Handbook says** the extension must be applied for before or on the day the candidacy ends.

Source: Postgraduate Student Handbook 2025, section 2.1.1, p.8 (Handbook; verify page); the "expiry day allowed" rule: CGS process.
