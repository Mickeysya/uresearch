**CGS process, Candidacy Management > Operations.** The cards (In progress, Returned, Approved, Rejected) filter the list when clicked; the tabs split it the same way. Use Search (appeal number, student name or ID), Department, Status, Current step, Months requested, Waiting at least and Round to narrow it. Each row opens the appeal page read-only; CGS acts at the CGS verification step in the Approval Portal. The page updates by itself every 15 seconds (Pause updates to stop it).
