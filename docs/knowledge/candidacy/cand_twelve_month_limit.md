**CGS process.** The maximum extension is {{cand_max_extension_months}} months in total, across all your appeals together (for example 6 + 6, or 3 + 4 and 5 still left). You can see how many months you have used and have left on My candidacy. When all {{cand_max_extension_months}} months are used, no further appeal can be submitted.

The source of the {{cand_max_extension_months}}-month limit is being confirmed with CGS (the Handbook sections read so far do not state the number).
