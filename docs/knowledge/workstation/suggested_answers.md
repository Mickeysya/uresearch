### s1_rules
Under the Postgraduate Workstation Term of Use:
• Access is Monday to Friday, 7:00 AM - 6:00 PM only. The last person to leave switches off the lights, fans and air-conditioners.
• Use the workstation for research and study only. No sleeping or cooking, and nothing unlawful or immoral.
• Do not modify the workstation, bring harmful devices or materials, or take UTP facilities out of the room.
• Report any damage or loss to CGS immediately. You may be billed if it is caused by negligence.
• CGS may inspect the workstations, and belongings left at an unauthorised workstation can be confiscated.
• Your loan can be terminated (for example deferment, change to coursework or part-time, graduation, withdrawal, dismissal, de-registration, disciplinary action, or absence without approval). You must then vacate within {{vacate_working_days}} working days of the notice.

### s4_change_workstation
Yes. Use "Return Workstation" on your Workstation page and choose a reason (for example "Changing to another workstation"), then select another available seat in your rooms and press Confirm in the box that opens. A seat you release can be taken by someone else, so choose carefully. If you collected a locker key, return it to CGS within {{return_period_days}} days; until you return it you cannot request a new key.

### s5_lost_key
Report it to CGS immediately (Term of Use, clause 6). CGS is open Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM (GMT +8.00). You may be held responsible for losses caused by negligence. CGS will advise you on the next steps.

### s6_get_key
First request the key on your Workstation page (available once your seat is confirmed). Then collect it at the Centre for Graduate Studies (CGS), Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM (GMT +8.00), within {{key_collection_days}} days of your request. Uncollected requests are cancelled automatically after {{key_collection_days}} days, and you can request again.

### c4_candidacy_expiry
The workstation loan ends at the candidacy expiry date (including any approved extension). If no appeal is pending, the system issues a termination notice with the reason "Loan period ended (candidacy expiry)". The student is emailed and has {{vacate_working_days}} working days to vacate. The seat stays Occupied with a "Vacate by" label and is released automatically after that date. A collected key becomes Return Pending and the seat becomes Awaiting Key Return; a pending key request is cancelled. You can use "Release now" or "Withdraw Termination" on the seat. If an appeal is pending, no notice is issued.

### c5_reassign_release
Open the seat (Block, Room, View Details, or use the search box), then use Actions.
• Reassign: choose an available seat of the same gender (any block), choose a reason, confirm. The student is emailed the new seat.
• Release: choose a reason and type the seat number to confirm. Termination reasons give the student {{vacate_working_days}} working days to vacate. "Immediate correction" reasons (System error, Allocation error, Student has vacated) release the seat at once.
• Awaiting Key Return seats: use "Mark as Returned", or "Release Hold" if the key is lost.

### c6_key_lost_cgs_side
The student must report the loss to CGS immediately (Term of Use, clause 6). In the system: if the student has already left and the seat shows Awaiting Key Return, open the seat and use Release Hold with the reason "Key lost"; the seat becomes Available and the key is marked Lost in History. If the student still holds the seat, the system has no lost-key action, so handle the replacement and any charges according to CGS procedure.
