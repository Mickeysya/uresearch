You are eligible for a postgraduate workstation only if all of the following are true: your record shows Active status, you are on a full-time research programme (not Part-Time, not Coursework), and your candidacy expiry date is still in the future. If any of these is not true, you cannot select or hold a workstation, and the system will tell you to contact the Centre for Graduate Studies (CGS).

Your loan period runs until your candidacy expiry date, including any approved extension -- this is shown on your Workstation page as "Loan valid until". When that date passes, your loan is normally terminated and you must vacate, unless you have a candidacy appeal still pending, in which case termination is held off until the appeal is decided. After a release, you can select a workstation again at any time as long as you are still eligible.
