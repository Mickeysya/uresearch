Collect your locker key, and return it, at the same place: Centre for Graduate Studies (CGS), Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM - 5:00 PM (GMT +8.00). Please refer to the seat layout displayed in the room to locate your own seat.

If your seat is released or you are reassigned while the key is already Collected, you still owe its return -- normally within {{return_period_days}} days of the release. You will get one reminder {{return_reminder_days_before}} days before the return is due. A key that is still out is shown as "Return Pending" (or "Overdue" once past its due date) until it is physically returned and marked as such by CGS.

You can still select a new workstation while a previous key is outstanding, but you cannot request the locker key for the new seat until the old one is returned.
