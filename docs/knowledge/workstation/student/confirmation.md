When you press Select Seat, a confirmation box opens in the page showing the seat, block and room. Press Confirm and the seat is yours straight away; press Cancel (or Escape, or click outside the box) and nothing changes. There is no time window and no confirmation e-mail: you see a success message on the page and a notice in your bell. By pressing Confirm you also agree to the Postgraduate Workstation Term of Use.

If someone else took the seat first (for example, the page was still showing it as free), you see "This seat is no longer available. No change was made." at the top of the page, the list refreshes, and you can choose another seat. A seat is never given to two students.

If you do not see the seat you expected, check the notice in your bell and your Workstation page. If something still looks wrong, contact the Centre for Graduate Studies (CGS).
