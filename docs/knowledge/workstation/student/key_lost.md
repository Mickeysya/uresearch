If you lose your locker key, report it to the Centre for Graduate Studies (CGS) immediately -- do not wait. This is required under the Postgraduate Workstation Term of Use, clause 6: any damage or loss must be reported to CGS straight away, and you may be billed for the repair or replacement cost if it is due to negligence.

CGS can mark the key as Lost on their side, which frees your seat from the "Awaiting Key Return" state. A key marked Lost does not block you from requesting a new one later.
