Your workstation loan can be terminated for reasons including: Deferment, Change programme to Coursework, Change programme mode to Part-Time, Graduated, Withdrawn, Dismissal, De-registered, Under disciplinary action, Absent without prior approval, Other reason deemed appropriate by the University, or your loan period ending (your candidacy expiring).

When a termination notice is issued, your seat stays assigned to you, but you must vacate within {{vacate_working_days}} working days of the notice (Monday to Friday). You will see a banner and a vacate-by date on your Workstation page, and an email. If you have not vacated by the end of that day, the seat is released automatically. During the notice period you may still return the workstation yourself at any time, but you cannot select a different seat while this one is still assigned to you. A termination notice can be withdrawn by CGS, in which case you keep the seat and are notified that you may continue using it.
