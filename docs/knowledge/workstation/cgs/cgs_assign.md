Assign Student is only for a student who has no seat at all -- a student who already holds one (one student holds only one seat) is moved with Reassign instead, never Assign Student.

On an Available seat's Seat Details page, choose Assign Student. Search by name, student ID or email -- only students eligible for a workstation (Active, full-time research, candidacy not expired (the expiry day itself is still valid)), of the matching gender as the room, and who do not already hold a seat, are offered. Pick a student, a reason (Student unable to use the system, System error, Special case approved by CGS, or Other), and an optional remark. The student is notified by email that a seat has been assigned.
