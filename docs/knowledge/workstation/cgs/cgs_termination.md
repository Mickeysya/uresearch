A seat already in its termination notice period shows two actions instead of Reassign/Release:

"Release now" ends the loan immediately instead of waiting for the vacate-by date -- reason is Student has vacated or Other, and you must type the seat number to confirm.

"Withdraw Termination" cancels the notice -- the seat stays Occupied, the student is notified they may continue using it. A reason is required: Issued by mistake, Student situation changed, or Other.

Seats currently in a termination notice period can be filtered from the Workstation Management landing page using the Seat Status filter ("Termination notice"), and the free-text search also finds them by their reason.
