Everything the workstation system does follows the Postgraduate Workstation Term of Use (First Edition): eligibility (Active, full-time research, candidacy not expired), a seat that becomes the student's as soon as they press Confirm in the selection box, {{key_collection_days}} days to collect a requested locker key (reminder after {{key_collection_reminder_days}} days), {{return_period_days}} days to return a Collected key after a non-automatic release, and a {{vacate_working_days}}-working-day vacate period once a termination notice is issued. Where an older manual process (a paper form, a different office hour, a different approval step) conflicts with what this system actually does, the system's own rules win -- the Term of Use page has the full text.
