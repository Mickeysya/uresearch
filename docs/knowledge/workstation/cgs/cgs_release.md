On a seat's Seat Details page, Release has two reason groups:

Termination (5 working days notice): Deferment, Change programme to Coursework, Change programme mode to Part-Time, Graduated, Withdrawn, Dismissal, De-registered, Under disciplinary action, Absent without prior approval, or Other reason deemed appropriate by the University. Choosing one of these starts a termination notice -- the seat stays Occupied, the student is notified and must vacate within {{vacate_working_days}} working days, and the seat releases automatically after that.

Immediate correction: System error, Allocation error, or Student has vacated. Choosing one of these releases the seat right away, with the usual key handling. No email is sent for "Student has vacated" specifically, since the student has already left.

Either way you must type the seat number to confirm, and you may add an optional remark.
