The Postgraduate Workstation Term of Use (First Edition) is the set of rules that governs every workstation loan: how a seat is requested, what occupants may and may not do, CGS's right to inspect and to terminate a loan, and the reasons and process for termination. You agree to it by confirming a seat selection or by using an assigned workstation.

The full text, numbered clause by clause, is available on the "Postgraduate Workstation Term of Use" page, linked from the Workstation page and from every workstation-related email. A downloadable copy is available from that same page.
