After you select a seat, you receive an email with a confirmation link. The link is valid for {{confirmation_minutes}} minutes from the moment you select the seat. Clicking it is what actually gives you the seat -- selecting alone only holds it.

If you do not confirm within {{confirmation_minutes}} minutes, the hold expires automatically, the seat becomes Available again for other students, and you will need to select a seat again from the start. By clicking the confirmation link you also agree to the Postgraduate Workstation Term of Use.

If no email arrives: check your spam folder first. If you still do not have it after a few minutes, your hold will simply expire on its own and you can select the seat again (or a different one) once it does. If this keeps happening, contact the Centre for Graduate Studies (CGS).
