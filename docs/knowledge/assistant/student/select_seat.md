To select a workstation: go to Workstation, choose a block, choose a room (only rooms matching your own gender are shown), then choose an Available seat. Selecting a seat does not give it to you yet -- it holds the seat for {{confirmation_minutes}} minutes and emails you a confirmation link. You must click that link within {{confirmation_minutes}} minutes, or the hold expires and the seat becomes available to other students again. You can only hold or confirm one workstation at a time.

You must be an Active, full-time research student with a candidacy that has not expired to select a workstation. If you are not eligible, the system will tell you and you should contact the Centre for Graduate Studies (CGS).
