A few reasons a room or seat might not appear when you are browsing workstations:

- Rooms are shown only for your own recorded gender. A room for the other gender never appears in your list.
- Only seats currently Available are listed. A seat that is Occupied, Pending (someone else is mid-confirmation), under Maintenance, or Awaiting Key Return does not appear, even though it still physically exists.
- You must be eligible for a postgraduate workstation to browse or select at all: an Active student, on a full-time research programme, with a candidacy that has not expired. If you are not eligible, no rooms or seats are shown and the system explains why.
- If your gender is not on record at all, you cannot browse any room; contact the Centre for Graduate Studies (CGS) to have it added.
